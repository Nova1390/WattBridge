# Glossary

## Adapter

A module that connects to one external system and translates vendor-specific APIs into WattBridge contracts.

## Audit Log

A durable record of recommendations, skipped actions, approvals, rejections, and executed commands.

## Consumption

Current household power demand.

## Device Capability

A normalized description of what a device can report or do, such as current power, energy total, on/off command, mode selection, or temperature setpoint.

## Energy

Accumulated power over time. Usually measured in Wh or kWh.

## Export

Power flowing from the home to the electrical grid.

## Import

Power flowing from the electrical grid to the home.

## Manual Approval Mode

Automation mode where WattBridge can recommend an action, but a user must approve before any command is sent.

## Power

Instantaneous rate of energy use or production. Measured in watts.

## Production

Power generated by the photovoltaic system.

## Surplus

Available photovoltaic power after household consumption. In the initial model, surplus is closely related to grid export, subject to measurement quality and timing.

## Time-Series Sample

A timestamped measurement used for charts, aggregation, and rule evaluation.
